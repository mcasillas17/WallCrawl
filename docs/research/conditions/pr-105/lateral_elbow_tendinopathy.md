# Lateral elbow tendinopathy: literature review

**Overall evidence grade for exercise guidance: low.** Evidence for any one isometric hold time is weaker still. This is general information for a workout app. It is not diagnosis or treatment.

## Key points

- **Exercise helps a little, mostly early.** The Cochrane review found a small benefit at the end of treatment that may not be clinically worthwhile and does not last ([Wallis 2024](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD013042.pub2)).
- **No hold time has been shown to be best.** Published isometric doses range from 10 s to 60 s, and their dose "is based on clinical experience and varies among studies" ([Stasinopoulos 2022a](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm12010094)).
- **The default in this record is up to 60 s, five times, once daily.** This is the only dose set by a consensus process for routine treatment ([Bateman 2021](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-053841)), but it was set by vote, and its package showed similar improvement to usual physiotherapy in an unpowered pilot ([Bateman 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.msksp.2023.102905)).
- **Do not promise instant relief.** Isometric work increased pain immediately in this condition when done above pain threshold or as gripping ([Coombes 2016](https://doi.org/10.1097/AJP.0000000000000365), [Coombes 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.msksp.2025.103421)).
- **Guidelines disagree.** One national society does not support exercise-based physiotherapy at all ([Lapner 2022](https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.019221)), while others recommend it ([Lucado 2022](https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2022.0302), [Singh 2023](https://doi.org/10.1177/17585732231170793)).
- **Most people improve anyway.** Physiotherapy and wait-and-see converge by one year ([Bisset 2006](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.38961.584653.AE), [Smidt 2002](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(02)07811-X), [Coombes 2013](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2013.129)).

## Scope and search log

**Question:** which exercises and exact doses, especially isometric hold times, have support for adults with lateral elbow tendinopathy, and which movements should a workout planner modify?

**Method:** three independent searchers ran 44 logged queries and direct fetches across PubMed, Europe PMC, WebSearch and publisher sites. Results were merged by DOI and PMID. All PubMed identifiers were then matched against PubMed records, which also completed citations the searchers could not capture. The dose passages of [Bateman 2021](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-053841), [Clifford 2020](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjsem-2020-000760), [Stasinopoulos 2022a](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm12010094) and [Park 2010](https://doi.org/10.4055/cios.2010.2.3.173) were re-read in full text during the merge.

**What could not be read:**

- NICE CKS returned HTTP 403, and the original German AWMF guideline returned HTTP 500 or an HTML page. Only its published summary was read ([Leschinger 2022](https://doi.org/10.1055/a-1340-0931)).
- No Dutch guideline or AAOS lateral elbow guideline was located.
- The JOA ([Amako 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jos.2021.09.003)) and ACOEM ([Hegmann 2013](https://doi.org/10.1097/JOM.0b013e3182a0d7ec)) recommendations were read only at abstract level, so no grades are quoted from them.
- The Cochrane Library blocked direct access. Cochrane reviews were read as abstracts through PubMed or PMC.
- [Vuvan 2020](https://doi.org/10.1249/mss.0000000000002128) is paywalled, so its hold time and sets remain unknown.
- [Barratt 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physio.2017.09.001) is a subscription article and its repository copy returned HTTP 403, so its protocol was read only at abstract level.

Reviews without pooled effects point the same way ([Menta 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmpt.2015.06.002), [Hoogvliet 2013](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2012-091990)). [Kinney 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcm.2023.04.004) reports that exercise reduced pain "regardless of dosage or type", and [Cooper 2023](https://doi.org/10.3310/TFWS2748) found exercise safe and beneficial across tendinopathies on low and very low-quality evidence. [Raman 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jht.2011.09.001) found "optimal dosing is not defined". Supervised exercise beat home exercise in a sequentially allocated trial ([Stasinopoulos 2010](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsm.2008.049759)), so selection bias is a concern.

### Natural history and landmark trials

| Trial | 6 weeks (or 4 weeks where stated) | One year |
|---|---|---|
| [Bisset 2006](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.38961.584653.AE), n=198 | Success: wait-and-see 27%, injection 78%, physiotherapy 65% | 90%, 68%, 94% |
| [Smidt 2002](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(02)07811-X), n=185 | Injection 92%, physiotherapy 47%, wait-and-see 32% | 69%, 91%, 83% |
| [Coombes 2013](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2013.129), n=165 | At 4 weeks, physiotherapy plus placebo injection 39% vs no physiotherapy 10%, RR 4.00 (99% CI 1.07 to 15.00) | Physiotherapy 91% vs 88%, RR 1.04 (99% CI 0.90 to 1.19) |

Corticosteroid worsened one-year recovery in [Coombes 2013](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2013.129) (83% vs 96%; recurrence 54% vs 12%), and [Olaussen 2013](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2013-003564) found a negative intermediate effect. Injection regression was 47 of 65 in [Bisset 2006](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.38961.584653.AE). Physiotherapy was the only option improving quality of life in the economic analysis of the 2013 trial, MD 0.035 (95% CI 0.003 to 0.068) ([Coombes 2016 economic](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2015-094729)). PRP and autologous blood probably give no clinically significant benefit over placebo: pain 0.16 points better on 0-10 (95% CI 0.60 better to 0.29 worse) with moderate certainty ([Karjalainen 2021](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD010951.pub2)).

### Isometric exercise

- **Against wait-and-see:** PRTEE SMD -0.92 (95% CI -1.58 to -0.26) and worst pain SMD -0.80 (95% CI -1.45 to -0.14) favoured exercise at 8 weeks. Global success, 29% vs 26%, and pain-free grip, SMD -0.33 (95% CI -0.97 to 0.30), did not. The authors say "it is doubtful if this form of exercise is efficacious as a sole treatment" ([Vuvan 2020](https://doi.org/10.1249/mss.0000000000002128)).
- **Against isotonic:** not superior for chronic tendinopathy, immediately or at 12 weeks or less, on limited evidence across tendons. Only two lateral elbow studies were included, with 74 people ([Clifford 2020](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjsem-2020-000760)).
- **Across tendons:** statistically significant pain and function improvement in only 3 of 13 trials, 3 rated good quality and 10 poor ([Soliman 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clinthera.2026.04.027)).
- **As part of a mixed program:** adding isometrics to eccentric-concentric work gave the largest effect ([Stasinopoulos 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jht.2016.09.001)), but that arm had 56 min of loading a session against 22 min ([Clifford 2020](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjsem-2020-000760)).
- **Immediate pain:** above-threshold holds raised pain during contraction by MD 1.0 on the NRS (95% CI 0.4 to 1.5), and it stayed higher than below-threshold holds at 30 minutes (P=0.043). Below-threshold holds matched control ([Coombes 2016](https://doi.org/10.1097/AJP.0000000000000365)).

### Eccentric and heavy loading

- **Pooled:** large short-term effects on pain, 1.12 (CI 0.31 to 1.93), and function, 1.22 (CI 0.25 to 2.18), with inconclusive long-term results ([Chen Z 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jht.2020.02.002)).
- **Added to adjuvant care:** VAS SMD -0.63 (95% CI -0.90 to -0.36) and strength SMD 1.05 (95% CI 0.78 to 1.33). Against concentric or isotonic work: VAS SMD -0.30 (95% CI -0.58 to -0.02), with no difference in strength or function ([Yoon 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm10173968)).
- **Single trials:** [Tyler 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jse.2010.04.041) found large gains in 21 people (DASH 76% vs 13%, P=.01). [Peterson 2014](https://doi.org/10.1177/0269215514527595) found faster pain regression than concentric work, HR 0.78 (95% CI 0.63 to 0.96), NNT 10, with no difference in function. [Wen 2011](https://doi.org/10.1177/1941738111409690) found no difference from stretching and modalities through 20 weeks. [Sevier 2015](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.967) found Astym beat eccentric exercise at 4 weeks.
- **Heavy slow resistance:** only 6 of 19 reached adequate adherence, and the authors called it unsuitable ([Sveinall 2024](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-085916)). [Divya Mary 2025](https://doi.org/10.1002/hcs2.70004) reported the reverse, in a small pilot with a 14-year age gap between groups.
- **Blood flow restriction:** low-load training favoured PRTEE at 6 and 12 weeks against sham, by -11.92 and -15.23 ([Karanasios 2022](https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2022.11211)). Later small trials were mixed on pain ([Park JH 2026](https://doi.org/10.12659/MSM.951969), [El Melhat 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/10538127261494053)). All need a cuff and supervision, so this is not a self-guided app option.

### Stretching, bracing and passive care

- **Stretching:** adding strengthening to home stretching gave no extra benefit, and the relative value of stretching against strengthening "remains unknown" ([Menta 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmpt.2015.06.002)).
- **Bracing:** no added benefit from a forearm band over 12 months ([Nishizuka 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jos.2016.11.011)). Reviews are inconclusive ([Struijs 2002](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD001821), [Dion 2017](https://doi.org/10.1097/AJP.0000000000000368)), and every interval quoted in [Shahabi 2020](https://doi.org/10.1177/0309364620930618) includes zero.
- **Friction massage:** null in the lateral elbow study, pain MD -6.60 on 0-100 (95% CI -28.60 to 15.40) ([Loew 2014](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD003528.pub2)).

**Pain limits in use:**

- **Should not exacerbate symptoms:** [Lucado 2022](https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2022.0302)
- **Pain-free:** [Park 2010](https://doi.org/10.4055/cios.2010.2.3.173), [Sevier 2015](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.967)
- **Below 4 on VAS, stop above 8:** [Stasinopoulos 2022a](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm12010094)
- **Pain the patient deems acceptable:** [Bateman 2021](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-053841). The trial protocol calls this a "novel aspect" ([Bateman 2022 protocol](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40814-022-01132-x)), and [Bateman 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.msksp.2023.102905) reported no related adverse events in a sample too small to detect harm.
- **Reduce weight if pain afterwards is not tolerable, affects sleep or lasts into the next session:** [Sveinall 2024](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-085916)

Progression benchmarks were mostly pain-based, and none was shown superior ([Ortega-Castillo 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.msksp.2022.102645)).

## Red flags

Stop elbow-loading exercise and seek urgent medical assessment for these ([NHS Highland](https://www.rightdecisions.scot.nhs.uk/tam-treatments-and-medicines-nhs-highland/adult-therapeutic-guidelines/orthopaedics/musculoskeletal-pathways-guidelines/)):

- Atypical mass or swelling
- Unremitting or increasing pain
- Night pain
- Feeling systemically unwell, malaise or weight loss

Get prompt assessment for progressive weakness when extending the fingers or wrist, which can point to posterior interosseous nerve compression ([Cha 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24570771/), [Lucado 2022](https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2022.0302)).

Get a professional evaluation if symptoms may come from the neck or a nerve, or do not fit the usual pattern ([Lucado 2022](https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2022.0302)). Trial exclusions show which presentations are untested in this evidence: recent significant trauma, inflammatory arthritis or gout, elbow osteoarthritis, neurological symptoms at onset, and neck pain and stiffness starting at a similar time ([Bateman 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.msksp.2023.102905)).

**New, not urgent:** get a clinical review if there is no improvement after 12-24 weeks of treatment. The BESS pathway treats that as a trigger for referral ([Singh 2023](https://doi.org/10.1177/17585732231170793)).

**Exercise harms were mild.** Adverse events were mild and transient with no serious events, 70/233 vs 19/224, RR 3.69 (95% CI 0.98 to 13.97; uncertain) ([Wallis 2024](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD013042.pub2)). [Lucado 2022](https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2022.0302) reports no exercise adverse effects in its studies. [Vuvan 2020](https://doi.org/10.1249/mss.0000000000002128) is cited second-hand by [Sveinall 2024](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-085916) as reporting that 14% of its exercise group worsened, which was not seen in the abstract.

## Contradictions and limitations

### Contradictions

1. **Whether to recommend exercise.** [Lucado 2022](https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2022.0302) and [Singh 2023](https://doi.org/10.1177/17585732231170793) recommend it. [Lapner 2022](https://doi.org/10.1503/cjs.019221) does not support it, though it excluded combination therapies and rated pain evidence low. [Wallis 2024](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD013042.pub2) says quality of life was worse with prescribed exercise in 2 trials with 113 people (MD -5.58, the abstract's wording).
2. **Pain during exercise.** See the pain limits above. No rule has been shown superior.
3. **Immediate analgesia.** [Coombes 2016](https://doi.org/10.1097/AJP.0000000000000365), [Coombes 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.msksp.2025.103421) and [Clifford 2020](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjsem-2020-000760) do not support it. Later results after one small volleyball study have been variable ([Clifford 2020](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjsem-2020-000760)).
4. **Bateman wording.** "Up to 60 s" in the results, and "maximal resistance, held for 60 s" in the discussion, attributed to [Barratt 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physio.2017.09.001), an uncontrolled audit whose abstract gives no hold time and whose full text could not be opened. One searcher reported the "maximal resistance" phrase as absent. The full text was re-read, and it is there ([Bateman 2021](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-053841)).
5. **Park wording.** The primary paper says four sets of 50 daily ([Park 2010](https://doi.org/10.4055/cios.2010.2.3.173)). [Bateman 2021](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-053841) and [Stasinopoulos 2022a](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm12010094) restate it as four times a day. The primary wording is used here.
6. **Stasinopoulos frequency.** Daily per [Stasinopoulos 2022a](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm12010094), and 5 treatments a week per the abstract of [Stasinopoulos 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jht.2016.09.001). The 45 s dose was never read in the trial itself.
7. **Vuvan success.** 29% vs 26% in [Vuvan 2020](https://doi.org/10.1249/mss.0000000000002128), and overall improvement of 86% vs 63% in [Clifford 2020](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjsem-2020-000760). Both are non-significant. A different threshold is likely but could not be confirmed.
8. **Heavy loading.** [Sveinall 2024](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-085916) against [Divya Mary 2025](https://doi.org/10.1002/hcs2.70004). The pilot's mean ages were 48.42 and 62.42, and its age table total does not match n.
9. **Manual therapy.** Grade B in [Lucado 2022](https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2022.0302), excluded in [Bateman 2021](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-053841) partly on cost and burden. Mobilisation-with-movement prevented the pain rise from gripping in [Coombes 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.msksp.2025.103421), but needs a therapist.
10. **Bracing.** Grade D and F in [Lucado 2022](https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2022.0302), conditional in [Singh 2023](https://doi.org/10.1177/17585732231170793), included in [Bateman 2021](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-053841), null in [Nishizuka 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jos.2016.11.011), short-term benefit in [Ouyang 2026](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2026.1782562).
11. **Injections.** [Kinney 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcm.2023.04.004) favours PRP and autologous blood long term, and [Karjalainen 2021](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD010951.pub2) finds no meaningful benefit at three months.
12. **Eccentric as a gold standard.** Challenged in [Stasinopoulos 2022b](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm11051325) and [Sevier 2015](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.967), and supported by [Tyler 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jse.2010.04.041) and [Peterson 2014](https://doi.org/10.1177/0269215514527595).
13. **Short-term network results.** [Lowdon 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhsa.2024.03.007) found nothing better than control up to six weeks. [Ouyang 2026](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2026.1782562) found taping, corticosteroid, brace and laser better at 1-4 weeks.
14. **Tool load figures.** [Lucado 2022](https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2022.0302) lists tools over 20 kg, and [van Rijn 2009](https://doi.org/10.1093/rheumatology/kep013) reports tools over 1 kg and loads over 20 kg.
15. **Sleep and night pain.** [Sveinall 2024](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-085916) treats sleep-disturbing pain after training as a cue to reduce weight, and [NHS Highland](https://www.rightdecisions.scot.nhs.uk/tam-treatments-and-medicines-nhs-highland/adult-therapeutic-guidelines/orthopaedics/musculoskeletal-pathways-guidelines/) lists night pain as a red flag. This record keeps the red flag for night pain not explained by a recent session. That split is this record's reading.

### Limitations

- **Dose:** [Lucado 2022](https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2022.0302) says no study described how load was determined or progressed. 71% of studies did not report adherence ([Taylor 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.msksp.2024.102978)), so dose-response cannot be inferred.
- **Fragility:** median fragility index 4, and 8 of 29 significant outcomes reversed with one event ([Shah 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xrrt.2025.03.008)).
- **Extrapolation:** the null search for gym-lift loading screened only the top 15 of 44 titles. Occupational and laboratory data are not gym data, and supinated or neutral grip advice is untested.
- **Access:** many sources were abstract only, and several full texts were read in targeted sections or through summarising tools, as the References show.
- **Numbers not reused:** the ESWT function interval in [Chen Q 2023](https://doi.org/10.2340/jphs.v58.12333) appears misprinted. [Coombes 2013](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2013.129) intervals are 99%, not 95%.
- **Red flags:** they come from one local pathway and one case-based article, not a validated tool. Both were reopened in the verification. Some patient-facing wording is this record's own.
- **Carried from the earlier check:** "Stop elbow-loading exercise" is this record's wording, since the NHS Highland action is a clinician-facing urgent call and referral. "Wrist" in the nerve flag is an anatomical reading of [Cha 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24570771/). The [Tyler 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jse.2010.04.041) 3 x 15 dose was checked only as tabulated in [Yoon 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm10173968).
- **Reversed earlier removals:** an earlier check removed a gripping restriction and the wrist extensor stretch as unsupported. Both return in narrower or sourced form, based on the sources above.

## Verification

An independent check covered all 66 sources: DOIs tested, PMIDs matched to titles, abstracts or full text read against claims.

**Removed:**

- avoidOrModify gripping: the 'Hard or sustained' qualifier. No source compares grip intensity or duration against symptoms beyond 15% and 30% of maximum in 11 people. The grip intensity in Coombes 2025 was not read. The item now covers gripping that provokes pain.
- Wrist extensor stretch, Sevier 2015: 'Lowest stretch dose found'. It rested on a wrong frequency. The trial used 3 stretches a day.
- Palm-down lifting: 'low-load' describing Di Domizio 2010. The abstract gives no load level for the push and pull tasks.
- Contradiction 3: volleyball results 'have not been replicated'. Clifford 2020 says later results were variable.

**Corrected:**

- Sevier 2015 stretch frequency: was tied to 2 sessions a week; the paper says 3 times daily, one 30 s repetition.
- Park JH 2026: type changed from controlled_trial to rct, because the paper states randomisation. The cuff applies only to the experimental arm.
- Barratt and Selfe: was reported as not found. It is Physiotherapy 2018;104(2):209-216, PMID 29366541, and was added as a source with abstract access.
- Wen 2011: resistance comes from the other hand, not a rubber bar.
- Martinez-Silvestrini 2005: load changed from not extracted to elastic resistance band, per the Yoon 2021 table.
- Wallis 2024 table row: 23 trials and 1612 people is the whole review; the pain estimate comes from 12 trials and 1023 people.
- Taylor 2024: 71% refers to 74 of 104 studies, not trials.
- Lucado 2022 grade B row: only rigid taping is limited to irritable cases, and dry needling is not limited to short-term relief.
- OPTimisE: changed from not better than usual physiotherapy to similar improvement in an unpowered pilot.
- Bateman 2022 protocol citation completed: 2022;8(1):178.
- accessLevel for nhs_highland and cha2014 changed from not_reconfirmed to full_text, because both were opened and support their red flags.

**Could not be verified:**

- Barratt 2018 hold time, repetitions and load: subscription article, repository copy returned 403. Bateman 2021 attribution of 60 s x 5 to it is unconfirmed.
- Vuvan 2020 hold time, sets and pain rule: paywalled, repository copy returned 403.
- Coombes 2025 grip intensity and Coombes 2016 load method: abstract only.
- Stasinopoulos 2017 45 s x 15: read only through Stasinopoulos 2022a.
- Tyler 2010 3 x 15: read only in the Yoon 2021 table.
- Sources marked abstract_only were checked against their PubMed abstract only.
- Singh 2023, Bisset 2006 and Kinney 2023 were checked through a summarising page fetch, because Europe PMC full-text XML returned HTTP 500.
- Lucado 2022 was read from the orthopt.org PDF. The publisher copy returned 403 earlier. The PDF is text-extracted, so table layout may be imperfect.
- Red flags: 'Stop elbow-loading exercise' is not in the NHS Highland pathway, whose action is an urgent call to on-call orthopaedics plus a referral. Cha 2014 names the digit extensors and extensor carpi radialis brevis, so 'wrist' is an anatomical reading.
