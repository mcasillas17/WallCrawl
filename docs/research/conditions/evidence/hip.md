## Hip osteoarthritis: Tailored activity with modest, comparator-dependent evidence

A positive exercise trial and a sham-controlled null result are retained alongside guideline advice and the current review. The draft implication is individualized exercise selection without promising large symptom benefits or imposing a diagnosis-wide lifting ban. PHOENIX floor bridge and chair sit-to-stand are execution candidates.
