Research/access date: 2026-11-01 America/Los_Angeles (2026-11-02 UTC session). Five dossiers are research drafts, all unreviewed. This is a focused evidence retrieval, not an exhaustive systematic review or clinical clearance. Exact source locations, protocols, access limits and rule chains are in [the five JSON dossiers](../).

## Corrections and limits that must survive promotion

- **LIFTMOR has a published correction.** Crossref's original-article relation identified [2019 erratum DOI 10.1002/jbmr.3659, PMID 30861219](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jbmr.3659). The notice corrects anthropometric/lifestyle Table 2 headings from percent to absolute change and the sign of the HiRIT calcium-intake change. It does not supply new fracture-prevention evidence. The extracted journal report is carried in the author's January 2019 thesis Chapter 5; it is not represented as the final corrected publisher PDF.
- **HSD protocol and manual disagree.** Main protocol describes a three-week familiarisation; the manual calls the first four weeks preparatory. Main modification criteria refer to persistent symptoms beyond 2–3 hours; manual asks return to baseline within 30 minutes/before next session. No harmonized numeric app rule was invented. The five exercise instructions are readable; embedded figures have not been independently visually inspected.
- **Short-term benefit is not durable superiority.** HSD's 16-week relative WOSI effect was below its predefined minimal important difference; its one-year relative effect was nonsignificant. Fibromyalgia strength advantages were not established on long-term self-report follow-up; combined tai-chi versus aerobic FIQR advantage was nonsignificant at 52 weeks.
- **PA in general is not every movement.** The [2026 EULAR update](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42036268/) removed disease-specific contraindications to physical activity *per se*, while retaining individual assessment, adaptation and competent delivery. It does not clear every load, range, impact or comorbidity combination. Its 24-hour symptom example is not a universal diagnostic stopping rule.

## Osteoporosis

[Draft dossier](../osteoporosis.json) — five sources, four execution requests.

The [2018 LIFTMOR trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28975661/) randomized 101 screened postmenopausal women with low bone mass to eight months of supervised resistance/impact training or low-load home exercise. [Author thesis Chapter 5, particularly printed pp.118–121](https://research-repository.griffith.edu.au/server/api/core/bitstreams/b6b80b27-44f2-4f82-a315-370c13c7e34f/content) supplies actual eligibility and delivery: twice weekly 30 minutes; first-month technique/low-load practice; later deadlift, back squat and overhead press, plus a jumping chin-up/drop landing. The BMD/function signal is a bundled, highly supervised comparison, not proof of reduced fractures or safety for unscreened app heavy lifting. One lower-back spasm was reported; the paper's broader safety wording must not erase it. Recent fracture, localized back pain and important comorbidities/surgery were excluded; these boundaries do not create universal contraindications.

The [2022 UK consensus](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9304091/) separates evidence from consensus. Box 3 recommends modifying sustained, repeated, end-range or excessive loaded spinal flexion and teaching hip hinge; it does not support an arbitrary lifting-weight ceiling or ban on smooth comfortable rotation. Box 1's lower-impact advice for vertebral/multiple fragility fractures is explicitly precautionary and based on theoretical risk. The [2026 DVO guideline](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12902-026-02194-1) adds current fracture/fall/frailty stratification. Its high dynamic spinal-load contraindication and its lower-stimulus supervised trunk advice belong to different contexts and must not become one diagnosis-only blacklist.

The direct historical harms lead, [Sinaki and Mikkelsen 1984, PMID 6487063](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6487063/), was initially abstract-only. The coordinator subsequently rendered and visually inspected all four [scanned original pages](https://therapilates.com/PDF/sinaki.pdf). This is a retrospective review of 59 women: exercise selection followed the treating physiatrist’s method, with unequal groups and follow-up. All groups received heat/massage and lifting/bending advice. Group N (neither flexion nor extension) specifically received posture instruction, and some group N participants also performed abdominal isometrics; these components were not separately tested. Radiographic differences therefore do not establish a universal causal ban or lifting cap. Sets, repetitions, hold times and cohort frequency remain unreported; frequency mentioned in the discussion belongs to another study. No new exercise identity was inferred.

Candidate ordinary-training implications are spine-sparing modification and fracture-specific impact review. Four LIFTMOR execution requests preserve known movements and missing depth/setup/landing detail. They are not accepted equivalents of catalog squat, deadlift, overhead press or chin-up.

## Rheumatoid arthritis

[Draft dossier](../rheumatoid-arthritis.json) — four sources, one execution request.

The [ACR guideline, published 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10947582/) strongly recommends exercise overall with moderate certainty, but resistance exercise is conditional with very-low certainty. Type/frequency/intensity/duration were not formally fixed; disease activity, damage/deformity, comorbidity and capabilities require tailoring. Complete web-rendered manuscript exercise sections were inspected; direct XML retrieval failed and direct HTML returned a challenge, which are separately recorded.

The [RAPIT 2003 randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/13130460/) is abstract-only here. Overall function improved over two years, but participants with considerable baseline large-joint damage may have had more structural progression. No full methods, damage-stratum tables or numeric threshold was obtained; the finding is not a ban on all resistance training or a hand/foot endpoint. Candidate loading caution is explicitly a product precaution pending that subgroup extraction.

The [2024 Bilberg multicentre trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11672065/) was retrieved as complete Europe PMC XML. Eighty-seven adults had treated stable RA, age 20–60, DAS28 below 5.1 and clinical/cardiopulmonary screening. Table 1 reports cycle-ergometer intervals plus individualized strength and a third aerobic session. Fitness improved, but pain and DAS28 between-group confidence intervals crossed zero. An arrhythmia episode required cardiology assessment; transient and persistent musculoskeletal pain led to changes in the program. Its pain/24-hour modification rule is retained as trial context, not a universal app threshold.

The full [EULAR 2026 publisher postprint](https://orbi.uliege.be/bitstream/2268/344456/1/Raush%20EULAR%20Physical%20activity%20recommendation%202026.pdf) was read, including Table 1 and recommendations 4–7. It supports adaptation and competent delivery. A [July 2026 exit meta-analysis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10067-026-08274-w) was inspected for currentness: it re-analyses older ACR evidence, finds possible rather than definitive stability, and supplies no new execution or safety clearance. It is recorded as contextual search evidence, not a counted primary trial.

The cycle-ergometer candidate is separate from resistance sets and retains screening/measurement gaps. Ordinary-training implications preserve individual symptom/flare review and known joint-damage uncertainty; no blanket loading ceiling is proposed.

## Axial spondyloarthritis

[Draft dossier](../axial-spondyloarthritis.json) — four sources, two execution requests.

The complete [2014 Sveaas pilot](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4182541/) supplies the actual endurance/strength protocol. Twenty-eight participants were randomized, with 24 in per-protocol analysis; moderate/high activity, stable TNF treatment and exercise-capable adults were represented. Treadmill intervals and six individually adapted strength movements were supervised twice weekly, with one additional endurance session. The primary ASDAS CI crossed zero, while several secondary fitness/function outcomes favored exercise. No observed adverse events among completers does not establish safety for ankylosed osteoporotic spines, fractures or every intensity.

The larger [100-patient trial, journal issue 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30745314/), has positive ASDAS/BASDAI effects in its accessible abstract. Its complete methods/harms were not obtained here, so its six protocol fields remain not reported; the 2014 execution is not falsely assigned to it. Secondary publications from that trial are not independent replication.

The [Korean College of Rheumatology 2026 update](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13619551/) is a final 1 October 2026 publication, inspected as complete XML. Recommendation 4-2 strongly recommends exercise but describes low certainty for disease activity/function; no one optimal protocol is established. The [2026 EULAR activity update](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42036268/) is also fully inspected. ASAS-EULAR's 2022 recommendation 4 was readable in indexed primary text, but publisher full retrieval returned 403; the record does not pretend full ASAS access. BSR 2025 and Spanish ESPOGUIA 2026 summaries were currentness leads, not separately extracted exact protocols. A future December 2026 BMJ proof (PMID 42716561) was excluded because publication before access was not verified.

The treadmill interval and unspecified abdominal stabilization bridge remain separate requests. The latter cannot be equated to a glute bridge or plank. There is no evidence-derived global intensity ceiling or global clearance; ankylosis, fracture, active enthesitis, cardiovascular status and surgery remain specific applicability gaps.

## Fibromyalgia

[Draft dossier](../fibromyalgia.json) — three sources, four execution requests.

The complete [Larsson 2015 trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4489359/) randomized 130 women to person-centered progressive resistance or relaxation, both with contextual care/stretching. Box 1 specifies machines, free weights, bodyweight elements and actual progression. Patient readiness could hold the load unchanged; only 42/67 reached 80% estimated 1RM. Short-term knee-extension strength, FIQ and pain signals coexist with five pain-related withdrawals, no handgrip group advantage and absent 13–18-month self-report superiority. No universal pain cap, fixed progression or durable benefit was invented.

The complete [Wang 2018 comparative trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5861462/) compared different tai-chi schedules with supervised aerobic training. Tai chi combines meditative movement, breathing and relaxation; aerobic training included stretching and graded choreography. At 24 weeks the combined FIQR difference was modest, while the matched-duration/frequency contrast was larger; the combined contrast was nonsignificant by 52 weeks. Attendance differed substantially. Twelve events were considered related, mostly minor musculoskeletal; ten serious events occurred but were judged unrelated. This is not evidence that an isolated catalog stretch reproduces the program.

The [Brazilian guideline](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s42358-025-00507-x) is final January 2026 despite a 2025 DOI suffix. Its current strength/aerobic recommendations and weaker flexibility evidence were inspected. Lower flexibility efficacy does not imply stretching harms or justify a strength-set substitution.

Three strength requests preserve missing machine/range/support detail; one Yang-style program request preserves unresolved forms and transitions. Individual tolerance/readiness review is a product interpretation of trial delivery/harms. No FM-wide load, range or exercise exclusion is established.

## Hypermobility spectrum disorder

[Draft dossier](../hypermobility-spectrum-disorder.json) — four sources, five execution requests.

The complete [2022 Shoulder-MOBILEX outcome report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9626913/) enrolled 100 generalized/historical HSD adults with persistent shoulder complaints; none had hEDS. Five dumbbell exercises with two supervised and one home session weekly were compared with a less-supervised low-load program. The modest short-term WOSI advantage was below its predefined MID; most secondary outcomes were inconclusive. Soreness/headache were more common, two HEAVY participants withdrew due to events, and one dislocation was recorded (causation not established). The complete [2024 one-year report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10982631/) did not establish relative superiority; it is the same cohort.

The complete [2020 protocol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7705859/) and [11-page HEAVY manual](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1186%2Fs13063-020-04892-0/MediaObjects/13063_2020_4892_MOESM3_ESM.pdf) provide precise text for side-lying external rotation with pillow, prone horizontal abduction with outward rotation, seated scaption, prone external rotation at 90° support, and supine straight-arm protraction. Manual printed pp.4–8 (PDF pp.5–9) contain these executions. Numeric symptom timing and familiarisation discrepancies are retained above. Manual back-twisting/extreme-position advice was shared education and not separately tested; it was not turned into an all-HSD rule.

The complete [April 2026 lower-limb systematic review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43166-026-00407-y) reports low-certainty heterogeneous pain/proprioception findings and no strength advantage. Mixed historical labels and ages 7–45 limit transfer to current adult HSD. Its design-classification inconsistencies are retained. PMID 41637690 is a separate scoping-review **protocol**, not results. No new lower-limb execution is manufactured from a review table.

Five new requests preserve the studied dumbbell/support/rotation mechanics. Catalog bodyweight prone raises do not verify these variants. No general stretching/end-range/heavy-loading ban or clearance follows.
