# Incorporating the merged condition-planning research

The merged work contributed a second search across eight conditions. Its 682 condition-specific records represent 654 distinct sources. Its reported access breakdown is 221 full texts, 408 abstracts, 22 registry or summary records and three sources the authors of the search could not reconfirm. The full text label includes targeted extraction. These totals describe the original search. They overlap the 50-dossier bibliography and should not be added to it. [PR 105](https://github.com/mcasillas17/WallCrawl/pull/105) · [Search and access report](../pr-105/CONSOLIDATION.md) · [Linked bibliography](../pr-105/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md)

Eight condition pages now offer this related reading. The app removes citations already listed on that page by DOI, PMID or URL and omits unreconfirmed records. The annotated 50-dossier bibliography keeps its narrower study populations and findings. Where an exercise also uses a teaching handout, the handout appears separately. No imported candidate routine or demand tag carries clinical release authorization.

The catalog displays neutral exercise descriptions. The study comparing glides plus a splint with splinting alone did not establish an additional between-group benefit. A photograph or chart can define a movement even when the trial does not establish a treatment effect. [Abdolrazaghi et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10035085/)

## Saved workouts and profiles

The merged PR and the earlier local implementation both used Room 17 and archive 7 for different data. Room 18 can open both schema layouts. Archive 8 accepts both historical v7 formats. Previously reported conditions remain visible in Profile and pause new planning until the report is addressed. No imported label creates clinical approval. Historical mobility keeps its role and per-repetition hold, including when read back from backup. A pain-stop can offer an exercise exclusion. Accepting changes the profile and the user can undo it in Profile. [Data and archive documentation](../../../privacy.md)
