## Two existing catalog matches

### Side-lying hip abduction

Side-Lying Leg Raise illustrates the supported-head, straight-knee lateral raise in both catalog variants. The lower hand supports the head and the elbow rests on the floor. The lower leg can remain straight in the study. The author asks for forward-facing toes throughout and gives no numeric height. Use the written cue for foot orientation, which the simplified drawings cannot establish precisely. The alternative Side-Lying Hip Abduction props the torso on the forearm and leaves the head unsupported.

[McKay et al., pilot randomized study, Table 2, exercise 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7247177/) · [SieuNarine-McKay, original UBC thesis, Table 5, group C, exercise 2, printed p. 45](https://central.bac-lac.gc.ca/.item?app=Library&id=TC-BVAU-58531&oclc_number=1032932654&op=pdf)

### PHOENIX bilateral floor bridge

Glute Bridge matches the bilateral exercise identity. PHOENIX starts on a firm surface with bent knees, feet flat and hip-width apart. Participants lift, pause for two to three seconds and lower slowly. The upstream and female sequences reach a low supported position. The male pelvis stays raised at its lowest frame. None of the drawings measures stance width or pause duration. An all-variant demonstration of the complete repetition needs a corrected lowered male endpoint.

[Hall et al., PHOENIX protocol](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12891-022-05282-0) · [PHOENIX Additional file 5, p. 1, Hip Extensors, Bridging, Bridge row](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1186%2Fs12891-022-05282-0/MediaObjects/12891_2022_5282_MOESM5_ESM.pdf)

### Single-leg machine knee-extension isometric at60-degree flexion

`knee-pt-single-leg-isometric-machine-extension` · **Specified**

Sit against the leg-extension machine backrest, hold the handles and place both lower legs under the padded lever. Use both legs to bring the load to a position with 60° of knee flexion. Take the other leg off the load and hold the lever still with the working leg. The study used 45-second holds, two minutes of rest and progression toward 70% of maximum voluntary contraction. Re-engage both legs to return the lever. The extension setup is in the written directions. The adjacent photographs demonstrate the press alternative.

- **Start**. Back against seat, hands on handles, both legs under the padded lever.
- **Middle**. Both legs move the lever to a 60° knee bend.
- **End**. The working leg alone holds that angle. Show the other leg clear of the lever.

### Single-leg machine leg press, staged range and load

`knee-pt-single-leg-isotonic-machine-press` · **Specified**

For the initial stage 2 range, sit with the full back supported, hands on handles and feet initially shoulder-width apart on the press plate. Work one leg at a time and leave the other at rest. Cycle under load between approximately 60° and 10° of knee flexion with a brief pause and a controlled return. The patient instructions later expand the travel toward 90° and near-straight as tolerated, with the knee kept out of lockout. This is the seated press shown in the manual.

- **Start**. Sit against the backrest, grip handles and set both feet on the plate.
- **Middle**. Working foot stays planted as the knee bends toward 60°, with the other leg off the plate.
- **End**. Press toward a 10° knee bend in the initial range, then repeat.

### Post ACLR open-chain knee extension, 90–45-degree clinical variant

`knee-acl-open-chain-extension-90-45` · **Specified**

For the supervised early open-chain arm of the hamstring-graft ACL reconstruction trial, sit on the knee-extension machine. Set the machine’s mechanical stop to limit movement to 90°–45° of knee flexion. From the 90° bent-knee position, extend the operated knee until 45° of flexion remains, then bend it back within the same arc. The training apparatus provided the limit. The early group began the restricted exercise in postoperative week four under the study therapists. Selection and timing in an individual surgical plan still depend on the procedure and clinician.

- **Start**. Seated at the knee-extension station, operated knee flexed 90° and machine range stop set.
- **Middle**. Move the lower leg forward against the machine through the restricted arc.
- **End**. Stop at 45° of knee flexion, then return toward 90°.

### Side-lying band-resisted eccentric-biased hip flexion

`iliopsoas-side-lying-band-eccentric` · **Specified**

In this case the runner lay on her right side with her spine supported against a wall. A band from the left ankle to a sturdy anchor behind her at about knee height pulled the hip toward extension. Begin with the left hip fully flexed and the knee bent. Keep that knee bent and control the backward pull over three counts until the hip reaches its extended position.
