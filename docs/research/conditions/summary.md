# Condition research corpus

Working evidence review for personalized workouts. This inventory contains 50 candidate conditions; it is not a list of 50 supported app conditions or a prevalence ranking. All therapeutic mappings remain drafts. Independent source checks and corrections have been completed within documented access limits; qualified clinical review remains outstanding.

Each regional evidence report links the actual medical papers and guidelines inspected, distinguishes full-text from abstract-only access, and records study populations, interventions, outcomes, harms, conflicts and limits. A study protocol is not an approved app prescription. Unavailable details stay unknown; no restriction is justified merely because another exercise helped.

Current state: linked source reports cover 50 of 50 conditions; 50 structured dossiers have been reconciled. Independent source verification and clinical review are separate gates; the review packet records the completed regional passes and remaining access limits. This is a targeted evidence review, not an exhaustive systematic review.

The structured records contain **187 distinct publications, guidelines and notices**, used in 196 condition-specific source records: 118 full-text and 78 abstract-only/partial-access records. These are access counts, not evidence-quality grades; the same publication can inform multiple conditions. Each record states exactly what was inspected. The corpus contains 227 extracted findings and 79 draft rules. None is an activated workout restriction.

## Reading the evidence

- **Restriction-only drafts:** [femoroacetabular impingement](femoroacetabular-impingement-syndrome.json) and [lower-limb bone stress injury](lower-limb-bone-stress-injury.json) have proposed ordinary-workout boundaries but no supportive exercise mapping. Their clinical applicability remains unresolved.
- **No exact mapping or rule established:** [adult degenerative scoliosis](adult-degenerative-scoliosis.json) and [glenohumeral osteoarthritis](glenohumeral-osteoarthritis.json) have medical research records but insufficient execution-level support for a specific exercise or automatic restriction. They must not appear as supported merely because they were researched.
- **Gaps that prevent release:** missing exercise figures/handouts, abstract-only protocols, mixed diagnoses, combined-treatment effects, supervision requirements, unclear adverse-event reporting and unvalidated load/range thresholds are retained in each dossier's `gaps`, source limitations and mapping reasons. A zero in the table means no candidate was established, not that exercise is forbidden.
- Published recommendations, measured study findings and proposed product precautions are separate. Trial exclusions describe who was studied; they do not automatically establish a contraindication.
- Studies of surgical rehabilitation, acute trauma, chronic symptoms, different diagnoses or supervised care do not automatically transfer to ordinary unsupervised workouts.
- Full text is sought through publishers, PubMed/PMC and lawful repositories. An abstract-only source cannot establish an unseen exercise protocol.
- Scientific references include negative and conflicting results. Source correction/version checks are bounded, not a guarantee that no correction exists.
- The exercise-request file tracks exact candidate executions and PNG needs; it does not add clinically supported catalog entries.
- Every proposed restriction needs a source-to-finding-to-rule trail and applicability limits. Evidence gaps can leave a condition research-only.
