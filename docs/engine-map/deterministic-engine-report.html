How a workout gets decided

WallCrawl has no model runtime. A pure Kotlin pipeline turns a stored profile, a bundled catalog and bounded local history into one session. Hard rules decide what is legal, comparators decide what is chosen, and a validator rejects anything that breaks the contract. The same inputs and generation index always give the same plan.

Stage 2 · ExerciseEligibilityPolicy

Legality is decided before ranking

Every exercise gets a typed decision. If nothing survives, the failure names the first stage that emptied the pool, and the planner never falls back to unreviewed work.

Stage 3 · FakeWorkoutPlanner

Selection is a sorted list

The planner picks a split from high-priority muscles it can actually fill, falling back to the default rotation. The rotation advances with the completed-workout count, or the program slot. Session length sets the slot count: up to 35 minutes gives 3 exercises, up to 55 gives 5, and longer gives 6. Up to 3 slots are compounds, and at least one slot always stays an accessory.

Compound order

  1. Trains the split as its own purpose
  2. No modify demand
  3. No unresolved capability penalty
  4. Supported-regression preference
  5. No above-experience penalty
  6. Frequency and recency preference
  7. Higher legacy fatigue ordinal
  8. Stable exercise ID

Accessory order

  1. Trains the split as its own purpose
  2. No modify demand
  3. Isolation before compound
  4. Has programming metadata
  5. No unresolved capability penalty
  6. Supported-regression preference
  7. No above-experience penalty
  8. Frequency and recency, fatigue, then ID

Compounds also spread across movement patterns, one per pattern first, with a fallback to repeats. isStrengthWork() returns false for all 14 catalog stretches, so they can never enter the compound or accessory slots. A modify demand is a yes or no, never a count, and the exercise still appears when nothing better fits, with a cue. Stretches reach a session only through the separate mobility block, which holds at most three items from the reported conditions' routines, rotates with the split seed and never fails a plan.

Stage 3 · Prescription

Dose can only shrink

DefaultExercisePrescriptionFactory builds a base prescription and applies progression or an accepted deload. StateBasedTrainingPolicy then caps it and re-checks the capability and condition rules, so a gap in the gate cannot let a restriction through. It never invents a load: with no history or confirmed baseline, the load stays null.

The states production derives today, under the default V2 policy. BUILD and DEVELOP are defined at 12 weekly and 4 per exercise, but are not currently derived.

One-variable progression

Two latest comparable attempts must be fully completed, confirmed manageable and carry qualifying effort. Then exactly one axis moves: +2.5 kg, +1 rep range, −2.5 kg assistance, or +5 seconds. Weight steps convert to the user's unit. The engine also defines +25 m for distance, but distance work is not admitted to automatic planning.

Rest classes

Short, moderate and long map to 60, 90 and 180 seconds. An explicit stored user preference wins.

Stage 4 · ProgramValidator

Nothing is shown until the whole plan passes

  • Structure

    IDs exist, sit in the candidate set and match the exercise type.

  • Truthful labels

    The advertised split and each focus muscle must be trained by the session.

  • Declared constraints

    Exclusions, unique IDs, and any declared families or patterns.

  • Load provenance

    Every number traces to a held source or an authorized progression.

  • Duration agreement

    The estimate must be in bounds and within one minute of the shared estimator.

  • Weekly dose

    Completed plus proposed sets must fit the allowance, per direct primary muscle.

If the only problem is an exceeded allowance, one repair pass may reduce targetSets, keeping at least one set per exercise. A repaired exercise also reverts to its pre-progression reference and records HOLD_VALIDATION_REPAIR. Repair cannot add, drop or swap exercises. At start, TodayViewModel rebuilds the context, compares the digest and revalidates with repair off, so a stale plan is reported instead of started.

Worked trace

What happens if you report piriformis syndrome

A reported condition is chosen from a fixed list; the app asks no other medical question. This trace follows the shipped overlay.

// profile.reportedConditions = {piriformis_syndrome}, full gym restrictionsFor Active(avoid = {}, modify = 10 demands) ExerciseEligibilityPolicy decisionFor(id) is null for reviewed exercises // nothing avoided CONDITION_DEMANDS_UNREVIEWED only for an exercise with no overlay entry FakeWorkoutPlanner a modify exercise ranks after a clean one, and still appears if nothing fits MobilityBlockPolicy figure-four, lying stretch, glute bridge, rotated by the seed, at most 3 ProgramValidator checks the block; a violation is typed and not repairable Result a normal plan, plus a block labelled as general information

Nothing here says the plan is safe for the condition. A condition with an avoid demand removes only the exercises carrying it, and a profile whose avoid demands remove every candidate gets the typed CONDITIONS_REMOVED_ALL refusal. No shipped condition does. A joint label such as HIP_SENSITIVE now only ranks exercises lower, and never switches automatic planning off.

Condition seams

Where conditions plug in, and what remains open

Profile

reportedConditions is a set of ids from the shipped list, stored in Room schema 17 and archive format 7, and part of the context digest.

Open

Conditions are health-related information. They stay on the device under the backup exclusions and appear in a user-chosen export.

Overlay

Bundled condition-guidance.json holds 21 categorical demands, eight conditions and joint-label demands, with AI review provenance. A load failure fails closed.

Open

A qualified clinician must review all overlay content, including red-flag tiers and owned setup wording, before any public release.

Session shape

The mobility block is separate from compound and accessory slots. Entries carry the MOBILITY role and earn no ledger, scheduling, progression, focus or analytics credit.

Open

Evidence is weak overall: 654 linked sources, most read as abstract only. The app makes no benefit claim.

Catalog

Seven owned mobility exercises join the 302 upstream entries, 309 in all, under MIT as original work.

Open

An illustrator must redraw the figure-four, lying piriformis and side-lying hold art, and the owner must confirm the MIT wording.

Feedback

After a PAIN_STOP the logger offers to exclude that exercise. Profile undoes it. The planner never reads PAIN_STOP itself.

Open

Nothing is inferred from repeated stops, by design.

Manual templates

Templates still allow every exercise and warn beside an avoid, modify or unreviewed one. Nothing is hidden.

Open

Scaling past eight conditions is a separate decision; the pilot criterion failed narrowly.

Recorded limits

Boundaries this feature has to change or respect

Exclude medical, injury-prevention, diagnostic, and unsupported performance claims.ROADMAP.md, Package 11 (claims exclusion kept; condition content allowed)
A clearance is a reviewer's product judgement about a self-reported label; it is not a diagnosis, and reviewer agreement is not clinical validation.docs/reviewed-capability-eligibility.md:122-124

SafetyCopyTest fails the build on injury, prevention, healing and joint-protection vocabulary in either language. The evidence review also rules out numeric joint-stress and injury-risk scores. The 2026-11-01 decision in ROADMAP.md keeps the claims exclusion and allows condition names and condition-linked routines as general information.
